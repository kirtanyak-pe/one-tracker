**App name (30):** One Tracker: Expense Tracker

**Short description (80):**
Tracks spending from your bank SMS, on your phone only. No sign-up, no internet.

**Full description:**

One Tracker turns the alerts your bank already sends you into a clear picture
of where your money goes — automatically, and entirely on your phone.

• Automatic: reads bank, card and UPI alerts from SMS and payment-app
  notifications, and files each payment by merchant and category.
• Private by design: no account, no sign-up, and no internet access at all.
  Your messages and spending never leave your phone.
• Insights: monthly totals, categories, recurring payments and trends.
• Split with friends: mark a payment as shared and hand it to the Splitwise
  app in one tap (Splitwise app required).
• App lock with fingerprint or face, light and dark themes, home-screen widget.
• Your own backup: export to a file you choose and restore it any time.

Only bank and payment alerts are used; other messages and notifications are
skipped. You can deny SMS access and add expenses yourself.

One Tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Splitwise, Inc.
or any bank.
