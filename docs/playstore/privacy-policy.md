# One Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

One Tracker is an expense tracker that works entirely on your phone. It has no
internet access, no accounts and no analytics.

## What the app reads, and why

- **SMS** (with your permission): bank, card and UPI transaction alerts, to
  record your spending. Other messages are skipped and not stored.
- **Notifications** (with notification access): payment notifications from UPI
  and bank apps, to catch payments that send no SMS. Other notifications are
  skipped and not stored.
- **Contacts** (optional, with your permission): names, numbers and photos, to
  show a photo next to payments to people you know.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. Everything is processed and stored on your phone. The app cannot
connect to the internet, and it does not send, sell or share any data with us
or anyone else. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

If you use **Backup** in Settings, the app writes a file to a location you
choose; what happens to that file is up to you. If you use **Split**, the app
opens the Splitwise app with the amount and description you confirm; Splitwise's
own privacy policy applies to what you save there.

## Your control

You can turn off any permission in Android settings at any time. Uninstalling
the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
